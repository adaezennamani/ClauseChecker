How ClauseCheck works

A combination of fast rule based pattern matching and AI review.

1
Upload your document

Drop in a .pdf, .docx, or .txt contract. Everything below runs automatically.

2
Text extraction

The document is uploaded and converted to plain text so the analyzer has something to read.

3
Rule-based scan

A set of regex patterns is used to identify common risky phrases, such as one-sided termination clauses, broad indemnity terms, unclear notice periods, and other potentially problematic wording.

Rule-based
4
Gemini contextual analysis

Gemini reads the full document and flags ambiguous or risky clauses the regex patterns would miss.

AI-based
5
Results

Flagged terms are highlighted directly in your document, listed with severity and plain-language explanations, summarized in a risk chart, and exportable as a report.

Why hybrid? Regex is fast and consistent but only catches what it's explicitly told to look for. Gemini understands context but is slower and less predictable. Combining both gives broader coverage than either alone.
